# Zombies With Ender Pearls (Forge 1.20.1)

Gives zombies and undead variants the ability to spawn equipped with Ender Pearls, using tactical teleportation to ambush players and close combat gaps!

---

## 🌟 Features

* **Ender Pearl Combat AI**: When players attempt to flee, tower up, or kite at range, pearl-bearing zombies throw an Ender Pearl to instantly teleport behind or beside their target.
* **Supported Mobs**: Vanilla Zombies, Husks, Drowned, Zombie Villagers, and Zombified Piglins.
* **Teleportation Safety & Cooldowns**: Built-in throw cooldowns and safe landing logic prevent mobs from repeatedly teleporting into suffocation blocks.
* **Configurable Spawn Rates**: Customize pearl spawn chances individually for each undead mob variant.
